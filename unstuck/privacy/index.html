Tinydo Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · Gokche Tech, Turkey · sengulfaruk1@gmail.com

Tinydo has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your tasks, routines, goals, wins and streak live on your device and, if iCloud sync is on, in your own private iCloud database, which only you can access; we cannot read it. We built the app so that we can run it without knowing who you are.

What leaves your device

Nothing goes to the AI without your permission. The first time Tiny would think with AI, the app explains what is sent and to whom, and asks. If you say no, Tiny uses built-in steps and nothing below marked "AI" is sent. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy → "Tiny thinks with AI".

On-device only

Calendar events (if you turn on "Show my calendar") are read on your device to draw your day; they are never sent anywhere. Voice input is converted to text on your device where supported and is not stored.

Tiny's memory (what you told Tiny, in your own words, and how your plans went) stays on your device and, if iCloud sync is on, in your private iCloud database. Tiny's notes and reminders are scheduled on your device; when and how often you open the app is used only on your device to time them and to send fewer when you don't need them. "Delete all my data" erases all of it.

What Tiny learns about your days. To suggest better times and better help, Tinydo keeps a private log on your device of what happened to the things you planned: done, moved to another day, stopped, or missed; what kind of thing it was (for example study or exercise); the part of the day; the kind of help you chose (for example "just two minutes"); and your answer to "How's your brain today?". The patterns are worked out on your device and shown to you in "What Tiny knows about you", where you can also erase them. This log is never sent to us or to the AI, it is not used for ads, and entries older than 180 days are deleted automatically. It describes your days, not you: Tinydo does not profile your personality or health.

If you write something that suggests you are in crisis, Tinydo shows ways to reach people who can help. It does not contact anyone on your behalf and does not store or report that you did this.

What we never collect

Name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, health data, advertising identifiers, or anything from other apps. We do not sell or share data with data brokers or advertisers, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's privacy policy. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Your rights

Because the data we hold cannot be linked to you, we cannot look up or export "your" data. You can turn off usage sharing in Settings at any time, and "Delete all my data" in Settings removes everything on the device including the install identifier. If you are in the EU/EEA, UK, Turkey (KVKK) or California, you have rights of access, deletion and objection; write to us and we will help within 30 days.

Children

Tinydo is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country) and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare (server hosting, United States/EU edge locations)
  • Anthropic (AI model that writes Tiny's answers, steps and plans, United States)
  • Apple (App Store, payments, crash diagnostics delivery)

Not medical advice

Tinydo is a productivity companion. It does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition, it is not a crisis service, and it is not a substitute for professional care.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will show a notice in the app before the change takes effect. Previous versions are available on request.
