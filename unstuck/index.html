Tinydo

A planning and focus companion for everyone who gets stuck. iPhone and iPad.

Stuck is not lazy. Tinydo turns the thing you can't start into a first step you can, stays with you while you take it, and follows up like a friend when it doesn't happen.

  • Tell Tiny what's heavy, get one small first step
  • Gentle routines and reminders, including meds and water
  • A visual timer on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
  • Study plans with a countdown to your exam
  • No account, no ads, no tracking
