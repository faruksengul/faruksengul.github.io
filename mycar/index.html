MyCar: a hands-free AI driving assistant and voice GPS for iPhone

MyCar is a voice-first AI driving assistant app for iPhone. Say “MyCar” to get turn-by-turn directions, find fuel or a pharmacy nearby, ask the AI co-driver a question, log your trips and record dashcam video. It works on the phone in any car, and on the car’s screen in cars with Apple CarPlay.

On the App Store, MyCar is listed as “MyCar: AI Assistant”. It speaks 33 languages and is made by independent developer Faruk Şengül.

What MyCar does

Navigation

Turn-by-turn directions with voice guidance on the iPhone and on the CarPlay screen, and on the instrument cluster or head-up display in cars that support it. Search by voice, set a whole street as the destination, and hear the weather at your destination when the route starts. MyCar re-checks your arrival time every 3 minutes and offers a faster road when it saves real time.

Voice and AI co-driver

Say “MyCar”, then speak: “Take me to the airport”, “Is there a pharmacy nearby?”, “Call Mum”, “Take a note”. Everyday commands for routes, nearby places, calls, notes, music and trip recording work without the cloud AI. With a paid plan and your permission, talk naturally with the AI co-driver, powered by Anthropic’s Claude.

Nearby, by voice

Fuel, EV charging, food, parking, grocery, restroom, pharmacy, hospital, ATM, car wash, mechanic and tire shop: 12 kinds of places, by voice, on the phone and on the CarPlay screen. Say “I’m running low on fuel” and MyCar offers the nearest station.

On the road

On the phone and the CarPlay map: your speed and the road’s speed limit, a speed alarm that is on by default and a mirrored HUD mode for the windshield. Fixed speed-camera alerts about 650 m ahead where the law allows them (off in Germany, Switzerland, France, Egypt and Saudi Arabia), a break reminder after 2 hours of driving, and a calm check-in after a hard brake.

Road alerts ahead

MyCar tells you early, by voice, what is coming up: a curve too sharp for your current speed, a lower speed limit, a school during school hours, a level crossing, a tunnel, a speed bump, a pedestrian crossing on a fast road and a toll plaza, plus ice, fog, heavy rain, snow or strong wind that sets in during the drive. It works with or without a route and never talks over a turn instruction; pick one of three levels or switch it off. Included in the MyCar plans. Road data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

New-driver mode

In your first year behind the wheel? Set your driving experience to “I just started” in Settings, and MyCar tells you more on the road: hospitals, playgrounds, bus stops, roundabouts, joining a motorway, stop and give-way signs and more. On a route, a calm driving coach reminds you early, before each turn, to check your mirror, signal and get into position, says a quiet word afterwards, and says less as you get the hang of it. Left and right follow the country’s traffic, including countries that drive on the left. The coach only reminds you: it cannot see your mirrors, your indicator or the traffic. Included in the MyCar plans.

Trips and dashcam

Trips are logged automatically when your iPhone connects to CarPlay (or through a car-Bluetooth Shortcuts automation you set up), with distance, time and a fuel estimate; mark business trips and export a mileage report as CSV. The dashcam records with the iPhone camera while MyCar is on screen, locks the clip after a severe brake, and exports a ±15 s evidence clip with time, speed and GPS stamped in.

Garage and AI Repair

Several vehicles with date and mileage reminders (oil, service, inspection, insurance, tires and more), a document wallet with expiry alerts, offline VIN decoding, US recall checks (NHTSA), a fuel log with cost per km, and your parking spot saved at the end of each trip. With a Bluetooth LE OBD2 adapter (not included), read live engine data and stored fault codes. AI Repair gives a first opinion from a description or a photo. It is not a mechanic.

Music and more

Apple Music control (full songs need an Apple Music subscription) and podcasts. 49 dashboard themes on the phone, a CarPlay dashboard widget, a Live Activity while recording, and Siri Shortcuts such as “MyCar, where is my car”.

Who MyCar is for

Drivers who want one hands-free app instead of switching between a map, a chatbot, a dashcam app and a mileage log: daily commuters, road-trippers, people who drive for work and need a mileage report, and car owners who want reminders for maintenance, inspection and insurance.

MyCar is a driving aid. You are always responsible for driving safely and following traffic laws.

Frequently asked questions

What is MyCar?

MyCar is a voice-first driving assistant app for iPhone. It combines turn-by-turn navigation, an AI co-driver you can talk to, nearby places by voice, a trip log, a garage with maintenance reminders and a dashcam in one app. It works on the phone in any car, and on the car’s screen in cars with Apple CarPlay. MyCar is an iPhone app; there is no Android version. On the App Store it is listed as “MyCar: AI Assistant”.

Does MyCar work without Apple CarPlay?

Yes. On the iPhone, MyCar works in any car: mount the phone and use its dashboard mode for navigation, voice commands, the trip log and the dashcam. The car-screen experience, with the map and the voice assistant on the car’s display, needs a car with Apple CarPlay. MyCar is an app, not an adapter or “AI box”: it needs no extra hardware and does not add CarPlay to a car that doesn’t have it.

What does MyCar show on the CarPlay screen?

In a car with Apple CarPlay, MyCar shows its map with turn-by-turn guidance, destination search, nearby places, your recent destinations and saved parking spots, and a voice screen with the mic and the “MyCar” wake word. In cars that support it, the next turns also appear on the instrument cluster or head-up display. MyCar holds Apple’s CarPlay entitlements for navigation apps and for voice-based conversational apps.

Can I use MyCar hands-free?

Yes. While MyCar is open on the phone, or while your iPhone is connected to CarPlay, say “MyCar” and then your request, for example “take me to the airport” or “is there a pharmacy nearby?”. After each reply the mic reopens, so you can keep talking. The wake word is recognized on the iPhone and needs your language’s on-device speech model; otherwise tap the mic. While music or other audio plays through CarPlay, the wake word pauses; tap the mic instead. Your voice recording is never sent to MyCar.

Which languages does MyCar support?

MyCar is available in 33 languages: English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Polish, Ukrainian, Romanian, Czech, Slovak, Croatian, Hungarian, Greek, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian (Bokmål), Finnish, Catalan, Hebrew, Indonesian, Malay, Thai, and Vietnamese. The app, the wake word, everyday voice commands and the AI co-driver’s replies follow the language your iPhone uses for MyCar.

Which AI does MyCar use?

MyCar’s AI co-driver uses Anthropic’s Claude models through MyCar’s own server. It is part of the paid plans and runs only after you allow it; you can switch “AI replies” off in Settings, and everyday voice commands keep working. The AI receives the text of your question, not your voice recording, and never your GPS coordinates. Its answers are spoken in a natural voice.

Can MyCar find a pharmacy, fuel or EV charging nearby by voice?

Yes. Ask “Is there a pharmacy nearby?” or just say “I’m running low on fuel”. MyCar finds 12 kinds of places (fuel, EV charging, food, parking, grocery, restroom, pharmacy, hospital, ATM, car wash, mechanic and tire shop), reads out the nearest ones and routes you to the one you pick, on the phone or on the CarPlay screen.

Does MyCar have a dashcam?

Yes. MyCar records road video with the iPhone’s rear camera while the app is on screen, with per-second speed and GPS. After a severe brake it locks the clip so it isn’t overwritten, and you can share a ±15 s evidence clip with time, speed and location stamped in, or an incident report PDF. Videos stay inside the app and are not uploaded. It records sound only if you switch that on, and it has no parking mode.

Does MyCar log trips and business mileage?

Yes. When your iPhone connects to CarPlay (or through a car-Bluetooth Shortcuts automation you set up), MyCar logs the trip automatically with distance, time and a fuel estimate, and saves your parking spot when you arrive. You can also start a trip by voice. Mark trips as business trips and export a mileage report as CSV. Trip records stay on your iPhone.

Does MyCar warn about speed cameras?

Where the law allows it, MyCar warns about fixed speed cameras about 650 m ahead, using OpenStreetMap data. It does not cover mobile, average-speed or red-light cameras, and the alerts are off in Germany, Switzerland, France, Egypt and Saudi Arabia. On the phone and the CarPlay map, MyCar also shows your speed and the speed limit. Always follow the posted limit.

Is MyCar good for new drivers?

Yes, for drivers in their first year. Set your driving experience to “I just started” in Settings, and MyCar adds alerts for hospitals, playgrounds, bus stops, roundabouts, motorway merges and stop and give-way signs to its usual road alerts. On a route, a calm coach reminds you before each turn to check your mirror, signal and get into position, then steps back as you gain confidence. It only reminds you: it cannot see your mirrors, your indicator or the traffic, and you are always responsible for driving safely. New-driver mode and road alerts are part of the MyCar plans.

What does the MyCar subscription include?

MyCar Pro, MyCar Plus and MyCar Max are monthly, auto-renewing App Store subscriptions. Each one unlocks MyCar’s driving features and the AI co-driver. The plans differ in how many AI replies they include; the map is Apple Maps on every plan. Prices for your country are shown in the App Store and in the app before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple ID; a subscription renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, and you can manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings. There are no ads.

Is my data sold?

No. MyCar does not sell or rent personal data, shows no ads, contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps. Trips, dashcam videos, contacts, notes and parking spots stay on your iPhone. You can delete everything MyCar’s server holds about your installation in Settings → Support → “Delete my data on the server”. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
